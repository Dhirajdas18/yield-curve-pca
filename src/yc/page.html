Indian yield curve PCA

The government bond yield curve moves in a few shapes: the whole curve up or down, steeper or flatter, and the middle against the ends. The lab finds those shapes with principal component analysis on RBI’s month-end yields from 1 to 30 years since 1996, for any years and maturities you pick.
